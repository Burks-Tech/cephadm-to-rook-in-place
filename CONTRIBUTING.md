# Questions and contributions

This is a write-up of one migration, not a maintained tool. Still, questions and corrections are welcome.

- **Questions:** open an issue. Say which Ceph and Rook versions you run and how your OSDs were created (`ceph-volume lvm` or raw). The answer is often "we did not test that", and we will say so.
- **Corrections:** if something here is wrong, or a Rook/Ceph release changed a behaviour we describe, an issue or pull request with a source link (code, release note, tracker) is the most useful thing you can send.
- **Your own attempt:** reports of an in-place migration, successful or not, are the most valuable contribution. A short write-up in an issue is enough: versions, cluster shape, what you followed, what broke.

Please do not post keys, fsids, internal hostnames or addresses in issues. Hashes and redacted output are fine.

Nothing here is supported by the Rook or Ceph projects; please do not take questions about this procedure to their trackers as if it were.
